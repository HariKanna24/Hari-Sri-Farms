Our story

A family farm, rooted in family.

Hari Sri Farms is a family-run organic farm in Mothepalayam, near Mettupalayam, founded and cared for by Senthil Kumar. The name honours the next generation, Haresh S and Srimathi S, who carry the farm's legacy forward.

Banana groves, coconut palms, and marigold beds have grown here side by side for generations, beneath the watchful presence of the village temple. We farm without chemical inputs, by hand, harvesting only when produce is naturally ready — respecting the soil, and never compromising on quality.

Today, our produce reaches families across Tamil Nadu, Mumbai, and Kerala. The reach has grown; the way we farm has not.

Seasonal spotlight

Seasonal Marigold

Every marigold on the farm starts like this — a single seedling, planted by hand, timed against the temple and harvest calendar so the beds bloom exactly when the season calls for them.

Grown for garlands, festival décor and temple offerings, our marigold is raised to order rather than kept in constant supply. It is also commonly planted alongside our banana crop, where it works as a beneficial intercrop and trap crop, helping to protect the surrounding plants. Enquire ahead of your festival dates to reserve a bloom window.
